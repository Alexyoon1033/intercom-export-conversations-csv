Privacy policy

Product: ChatVault: Conversation Transcript & CSV Exporter (for Intercom)
Effective date: November 22, 2026

Local execution summary. ChatVault runs 100% locally inside your web browser. We do not collect, transmit, store, or process your personal data, Intercom access tokens, customer records, or conversation transcripts on any developer server.

1. Local data processing

Conversation retrieval, HTML-to-plain-text cleanup, CSV/NDJSON/Markdown serialization, and disk streaming happen inside your local browser:

  • Direct API communication. Your browser sends HTTPS requests directly to official Intercom regional endpoints (https://api.intercom.io/*, https://api.eu.intercom.io/*, https://api.au.intercom.io/*) and Gumroad's license verification endpoint (https://api.gumroad.com/v2/licenses/verify).
  • Zero proxy servers. Zero bytes of conversation data pass through any intermediary server.
  • Local browser storage. Export progress checkpoints (pagination cursors and completed conversation IDs) and your license verification timestamp live only in your browser's local IndexedDB and chrome.storage.local. You can clear both with one click inside the workstation.

2. Chrome extension permissions

  • storage. Caches your Gumroad license verification timestamp locally for 7-day offline access.
  • host_permissions (api.intercom.io, api.eu.intercom.io, api.au.intercom.io, api.gumroad.com). Allows the dedicated extension tab to query your regional Intercom endpoint and verify Gumroad license keys directly from the browser.

3. Independent utility disclaimer

ChatVault is an independent third-party utility and is not affiliated with, endorsed, or sponsored by Intercom, Inc.

4. Support and refund policy

You can test workspace compatibility with the free 50-conversation live export and the 500-conversation sandbox dataset before purchase. If you encounter a technical defect, email the support address on your Gumroad receipt within 14 days for a full refund.
